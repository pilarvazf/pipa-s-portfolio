About

Pilar Vazquez

Pilar Vazquez is an Argentinian film composer based in Los Angeles, California. She recently graduated from Berklee College of Music with a double Major in Composition and Film and Media Scoring. During her studies, she worked as a librarian for the Berklee Motion Picture Orchestra, a 60+ piece ensemble. As a composer, her work has been performed by the Hypercube Ensemble, a New York based quartet, and the Boston Conservatory.
